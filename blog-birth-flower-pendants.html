What's Your Birth Flower?

A simple, symbolic way to personalise a resin pendant — one flower for every month of the year.

← Back to the Journal

Birthstones get all the attention, but flowers carry meaning too — and unlike a stone, a real flower can be pressed, set in resin, and worn exactly as it grew. If you're looking for a gift that feels personal without being complicated, your birth flower is a good place to start.

What is a birth flower?

Much like birthstones, each month of the year has a flower traditionally associated with it — a custom that goes back centuries in different cultures. A birth flower pendant works the same way a birthstone ring does: it's a small, personal marker of the month someone was born, without needing an explanation.

Birth flowers by month

  • January — Carnation
  • February — Violet
  • March — Daffodil
  • April — Daisy
  • May — Lily of the Valley
  • June — Rose
  • July — Larkspur
  • August — Gladiolus
  • September — Aster
  • October — Marigold
  • November — Chrysanthemum
  • December — Narcissus

We don't always have the exact bloom pressed and ready, but we work with what's closest in colour and shape, or with a flower that carries a similar meaning — just message us on WhatsApp and we'll talk through the options.

Turning a birth flower into a resin pendant

Once we know your month (or the flower you'd like), it goes through the same hand-poured process as every other piece — mixed, layered, cured, and polished over a few days. If you want the full behind-the-scenes version, we've written about it here: How Handmade Resin Jewelry Is Made. Browse our current resin pendants for a sense of shape and style, then message us to make one specific to a month.

A gift that needs no explanation

A birth flower pendant works for almost any occasion — a birthday, obviously, but also as a thoughtful add-on for a new mother, a close friend, or yourself. It sits well alongside other personalised pieces too, like our initial keychains. For more gifting ideas by occasion, see our Resin Jewelry Gift Guide.
