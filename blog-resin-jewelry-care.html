Care Tips

How to Care for Your Resin Jewelry

Simple habits that keep your resin pieces clear, scratch-free, and colourful for years to come.

← Back to the Journal

Handmade resin jewelry is more durable than it looks, but it's still a handcrafted material — a little care goes a long way toward keeping it looking as clear and colourful as the day it arrived. Here's what we tell every customer.

Keep it out of direct sunlight and heat

Prolonged exposure to strong sunlight or heat — think a hot car dashboard, or a sunny windowsill — can cause resin to yellow slightly over time. Store your pieces somewhere cool and shaded when you're not wearing them.

Avoid perfume, lotion, and alcohol-based products

Spray perfume and apply lotion or hand sanitizer before putting on your jewelry, not after. Alcohol-based products in particular can dull the glossy finish of resin over repeated contact.

Take it off before water, swimming, or showering

While a quick splash won't damage a piece, we'd still recommend removing your resin jewelry before showering, swimming, or washing dishes. Chlorine and prolonged soaking aren't good for the finish or the metal findings.

Store pieces separately

Resin can scratch if it rubs against harder materials like metal charms or gemstones. Keep pieces in a soft pouch, a lined jewelry box, or simply separated from each other in a drawer, rather than tossed together in one pile.

Clean with a soft, damp cloth

If a piece needs cleaning, a soft cloth lightly dampened with water is usually all it needs. Avoid harsh chemical cleaners — they're unnecessary and can dull the surface rather than help it.

Handle it gently

Resin is hard once cured, but it can crack under a sharp impact, like being dropped on a tile floor. Treat it the way you'd treat glass jewelry — sturdy for everyday wear, but not indestructible.

Follow these habits and a handmade piece — whether it's a resin earring, a keychain, or a bangle — should stay looking beautiful for years.
