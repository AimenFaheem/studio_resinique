Gift Guide

Resin Jewelry Gift Guide

What to buy her (or him, or yourself) for a birthday, a wedding, or just because — handmade resin picks for every occasion.

← Back to the Journal

Jewelry is one of the most reliable gifts you can give — but a handmade resin piece has something a shop-bought chain doesn't: a story. Every piece is poured by hand, in small batches, which makes it feel personal even before you've customised anything. Here's how to pick the right one.

For a birthday

Birthdays call for something colourful and a little bit fun. A pair of resin earrings or dangling jhumka in her favourite colour is an easy win — it's wearable the same day, and pressed flowers or gold leaf make each pair feel a little more special than a generic gift.

For a wedding or engagement

For milestone occasions, a resin pendant or a set of rings makes a thoughtful gift that feels a step above everyday jewelry, without the price tag of gold or gemstones. Preserved floral pieces are also a popular way to turn a bridal bouquet or mehndi flowers into a keepsake that lasts.

A personalised keepsake

If you want the gift to feel unmistakably theirs, go personalised. Our initial keychains — a single resin letter with pressed petals and gold leaf, often paired with a small heart charm — are one of the most requested custom gifts we make. We also take fully custom orders for a specific colour palette, initials, or a keepsake flower you want preserved.

A small thank-you or "just because" gift

Not every gift needs a big occasion. A single keychain or a small piece from our other accessories is an affordable, low-pressure way to say thank you, congratulations, or thinking of you — and it still arrives looking like a handmade gift, not an afterthought.

Treating yourself

It doesn't have to be for someone else. Plenty of our customers order a piece purely because a colour or flower caught their eye — a bangle or a stack of rings is a small, guilt-free way to treat yourself between bigger occasions.

Not sure which piece fits the person you're shopping for? Message us on WhatsApp and we're happy to help you pick — or read What Is Resin Jewelry? if you're buying resin for the first time.
