What Is Resin Jewelry?

A beginner's guide to what resin actually is, and why it's become such a popular way to make jewelry.

← Back to the Journal

If you've ever picked up a pair of earrings and wondered how a real flower ended up frozen inside clear, glass-like glass — you were probably looking at resin jewelry. It's one of the most popular ways for independent jewelry makers to create pieces that are colourful, durable, and completely one of a kind.

So, what actually is resin?

Resin jewelry is made from epoxy resin — a liquid that starts as two separate components, resin and hardener. When they're mixed together in the right ratio, a chemical reaction begins that slowly turns the liquid into a solid, glass-clear material over the course of a day or two. Before it hardens, it can be poured into a mould, coloured, and layered with almost anything you want suspended inside it.

Why jewelry makers love working with it

Resin gives makers a level of creative freedom that's hard to get with traditional jewelry materials like metal or stone:

  • Clarity — cured resin is completely transparent, so anything set inside it (a pressed petal, gold leaf, glitter) stays visible, like it's floating in glass.
  • Colour — resin can be tinted any shade, from soft pastels to deep jewel tones, and mixed to get marbled or swirled effects.
  • Lightweight — despite looking substantial, resin pieces are much lighter to wear than glass or solid metal of the same size.
  • One-of-a-kind results — because each piece is hand-poured, no two pieces ever turn out identically. The way a petal settles, or how the gold leaf catches the light, is different every time.

What can be set inside resin?

This is where the real personality comes in. Common inclusions are pressed real flowers, dried petals, gold or silver leaf, fine glitter, and tiny beads. This is what makes handmade resin earrings, pendants, and jhumka feel so different from mass-produced jewelry — every piece carries something real and specific inside it.

Is resin jewelry durable?

Yes, when it's cured properly and cared for well. Cured epoxy resin is hard, scratch-resistant, and holds up to everyday wear far better than people expect. It's not indestructible, though — like any handmade piece, it deserves a little care to keep it looking its best. We've written a full guide on that here: How to Care for Your Resin Jewelry.

Handmade resin jewelry has become especially popular in Pakistan over the last few years — it's affordable compared to gold or silver, endlessly customisable, and makes a genuinely thoughtful handmade gift for birthdays, weddings, and everyday occasions alike.
