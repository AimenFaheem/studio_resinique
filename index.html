Our Story

Made by hand, on a small bench by the window.

Studio Resinique began with a single mould and a fascination for the way light moves through resin. Every piece is still made the same way — mixed in tiny batches, layered over days, and finished with a slow, careful polish.

We forage and press our own flowers, blend custom pigments, and only use hypoallergenic, tarnish-resistant findings so your piece can be worn every day.

How It's Made

From pour to polish

  1. 01

    Gather & press

    Flowers and botanicals are hand-picked, pressed, and dried until perfectly preserved.

  2. 02

    Mix & pour

    Resin is measured by hand, tinted with custom pastel pigments, and poured in thin layers.

  3. 03

    Cure & set

    Each piece cures slowly over several days, then is sanded smooth and set with gold-fill findings.

  4. 04

    Polish & pack

    A final hand polish brings the shine, then it's wrapped and sent with a little care card.
