Good to Know

Privacy policy

A plain-language explanation of what information we collect and how we use it — nothing more complicated than that.

What we collect

When you place an order, we ask for your name, phone number, city, delivery address, and optionally your email and any order notes, so we can make and ship your piece correctly. If you message us through the contact form, we collect your name and whatever contact details or message you choose to share.

How we use it

Your order details are recorded in a private order sheet we use internally to track, pack, and ship your piece, and to follow up with you on WhatsApp if we have a question about your order. We don't use your information for marketing emails or newsletters, and we don't sell or share it with any third party beyond what's needed to fulfil your order (for example, writing your address on the courier label).

Payments

We don't store any card details. Payments are made directly by you via bank transfer or Easypaisa to the account shown at checkout, and you confirm payment by sending us a screenshot on WhatsApp.

Cookies & browser storage

This site uses your browser's local storage to remember what's in your cart between visits, and a small cookie to make sure our launch promo popup doesn't show more than once per visit. Neither is used for advertising or tracking you across other websites, and no analytics or ad-tracking scripts run on this site.

Third-party links

Our Instagram and TikTok links take you to those platforms, which have their own privacy policies and practices that we don't control.

Questions about your data

If you'd like to know what information we have on file for you, or want it removed, message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out directly.
